Build a modern single-user personal finance web application for me.

## 1. PRODUCT VISION

The app is a private Personal Finance / Personal CFO dashboard used by ONE PERSON only.

The goal is NOT to build a complicated accounting system.

The goal is to help me answer these questions quickly:

1. How much money do I have right now?
2. Where is my money?
3. How much did I spend this month?
4. What am I spending money on?
5. Am I spending more or less than my income?
6. How much can I safely spend?
7. How much am I saving each month?
8. How is my net worth changing?
9. What are my biggest financial commitments?
10. Am I on track toward my financial goals?

The UX should feel like a combination of:

* Personal CFO
* Premium banking dashboard
* Modern fintech app
* Minimal productivity app

Avoid making it feel like an accounting / ERP / Excel replacement.

# 35. CALCULATIONS

Implement these calculations centrally in utility functions.

Net Worth:

Total Assets - Total Liabilities

Savings:

Income - Expenses

Savings Rate:

Savings / Income

Monthly Burn:

Average monthly expenses

Emergency Fund:

Cash / Monthly Burn

Debt Ratio:

Total Debt / Net Worth

Budget Usage:

Category Spending / Category Budget

Investment Return:

(Current Value - Cost Basis) / Cost Basis

Do not duplicate financial calculations across components.

# 36. DATA CONSISTENCY

Important rules:

When a transaction is added:

1. Save transaction.
2. Update account balance.
3. Update category spending.
4. Update budget.
5. Update dashboard KPIs.
6. Update analytics.

When a transaction is edited or deleted, reverse the previous impact correctly.

Transfers between accounts must NOT count as income or expense.

This is important.

Example:

Techcombank → Vietcombank

Should change balances:

Techcombank -₫10M
Vietcombank +₫10M

But:

Income = unchanged
Expenses = unchanged
